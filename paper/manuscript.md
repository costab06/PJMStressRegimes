## 2. Exploratory Observations

Initial inspection suggests that stressed grid episodes need not share a common
univariate precursor. For example, sustained positive load-forecast error
characterized the period preceding the December 2022 Elliott emergency actions,
whereas demand remained below forecast at the onset of the July 2026 emergency
action. This heterogeneity motivates treating grid stress as a multivariate
system state rather than defining it through any individual operational
threshold.
